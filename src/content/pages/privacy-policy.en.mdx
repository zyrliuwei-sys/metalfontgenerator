## Introduction

metalfontgenerator ("we", "us", or "our") is an AI platform for creating 3D metallic lettering and dimensional typography. You can enter a word or phrase, describe the look you want, choose materials and lighting, and generate visual metal text concepts. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use metalfontgenerator.

## Information We Collect

1. **Account information** — Your name, email address, and authentication details when you create or use an account.
2. **Generation inputs** — Words, phrases, prompts, reference images, material selections, lighting preferences, aspect ratios, and other settings submitted for generation.
3. **Generated designs** — The AI images created from your inputs and the related generation history saved to your account.
4. **Usage and credit data** — Generation activity, credit consumption, plan information, and account actions needed to operate the service.
5. **Device and technical information** — Browser type, device type, operating system, approximate network information, and diagnostic logs.
6. **Cookies and preferences** — Cookies or similar technologies used for authentication, language, theme, security, and essential product preferences.
7. **Payment information** — Billing and transaction information handled by our payment providers. We do not store full payment card numbers on our servers.

## How We Use Information

- Provide, secure, maintain, and improve metalfontgenerator.
- Send generation inputs to the AI providers needed to create the requested metal lettering concepts.
- Save generated designs so signed-in users can view and download them later.
- Manage accounts, credits, subscriptions, payments, and transaction confirmations.
- Send service notices, security alerts, and support messages.
- Respond to questions, feedback, and support requests.
- Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, and security incidents.

## Generation Inputs and AI Outputs

Your words, prompts, reference images, and style settings are processed to provide the generation features you request. We do not sell your generation inputs or use them for unrelated advertising. Inputs may be transmitted to third-party AI infrastructure providers as necessary to complete a generation request. Please do not submit text, logos, images, or other material that you do not have the right to use.

Generated designs and their basic metadata may be saved to your account history so you can view and download them later. To request deletion of account data, generation inputs, or generated designs, contact us at the address below.

## Sharing of Information

We share information only as needed to operate the service, including with:

- **AI infrastructure providers** — To process text, image, and metal lettering generation requests.
- **Payment providers** — To process purchases, subscriptions, and refunds.
- **Authentication, storage, and email providers** — To support account access, file delivery, and transactional communication.
- **Service providers and advisors** — Where needed to maintain, secure, or improve the platform under appropriate confidentiality obligations.
- **Legal authorities** — When required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the public, or our rights.

We do not sell your personal information.

## Data Retention

We retain account and transaction information while your account is active or as needed for legal, security, accounting, and dispute-resolution purposes. Generated designs and related history are retained until you delete them, request deletion, or close your account, subject to applicable retention requirements.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, contact us at **support@metalfontgenerator.org**. We may need to verify your identity before completing the request.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encryption in transit, access controls, and security monitoring. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children's Privacy

metalfontgenerator is not directed to children under 13, or under 16 in jurisdictions where that higher age applies. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new revision date. Your continued use of metalfontgenerator after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

## Contact Us

For privacy questions or data requests, contact **support@metalfontgenerator.org**.
